💧 Water plan — built around your 1 L bottle

  • 🚰 Refill alarms: 12:45 PM & 4:45 PM — one 1 L bottle lasts ≈ 8 sips (≈ 4 hours).
  • ✅ On track if your urine is pale yellow. Drink extra on hot UAE days and after walking. Never force large amounts at once — sip steadily.
  • ⚕️ If you have a kidney, heart or blood-pressure condition, confirm your daily water target with your doctor.
